QUICK START GUIDE

Study smarter,
one task at a time.

Get comfortable with your planner in four simple steps. Everything works directly in your browser, with no account required.

01

Add a task

Type a task title, choose a category and select a due date. Press “Add task” and watch the item appear in your planner.

02

Keep things organised

Use the All, To do and Completed buttons to switch views. The search box finds tasks by their title or category.

03

Mark a task complete

Select the checkbox next to a finished task. Your progress circle and task totals update immediately.

04

Return whenever you like

StudyFlow saves tasks in this browser's localStorage. Use the “Restore example tasks” button to reset the demonstration, if needed.
